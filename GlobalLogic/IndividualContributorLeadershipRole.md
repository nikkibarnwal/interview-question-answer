**Answer (My Style):**

On a daily basis, my work is usually a mix of **individual contribution** and **leadership responsibilities**.

- **Around 70% time**, I focus on **individual contribution** — coding, reviewing PRs, solving bugs, improving performance, designing API contracts, and working on core features. Main yeh ensure karta hoon ki jo module mere paas hai, woh high-quality aur time-bound deliver ho.

- **Around 30% time**, I take up **leadership responsibilities** —
  • juniors ko guide karna,
  • unke doubts clear karna,
  • architecture discussion me inputs dena,
  • sprint planning & estimation me support,
  • and ensuring team blockers resolve ho jaye.

I try to balance both. Jab critical release hota hai, tab individual contribution thoda zyada ho jata hai. Aur jab new members join hote hain ya complex feature aa jata hai, tab leadership role thoda increase ho jata hai.

Overall goal simple hota hai:
**Team ko smooth rakhna + apni delivery strong rakhni.**
